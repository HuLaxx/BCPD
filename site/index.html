03 / THE MECHANISM

Keep multiple explanations alive.

Rather than selecting one changepoint, the model maintains probabilities for many possible run lengths—how long the present regime might have lasted. A constant hazard sets the prior chance of a reset. A conjugate Normal model updates the estimated segment mean for each hypothesis.

04 / THE LIMITS

Probability is not proof.

This implementation assumes Gaussian observations with fixed known variance. It detects mean changes, not every kind of regime shift. Autocorrelation, trends, seasonal signals and incorrect variance assumptions can mislead it. Treat peaks as questions for further investigation, not established events.
